The problem

Existing estate-inventory tools do not understand firearms.

Generic probate software treats a $48,000 transferable machine gun the same as a kitchen appliance. There's no NFA tax-stamp field, no Form 5 transferee tracking, no rare-variant detection, no chain-of-custody for items the court will scrutinize. Executors end up in spreadsheets, attorneys redraft inventories from photos, and FFLs charge five figures to "do the paperwork."

01

Misvaluation costs heirs.

A correctly identified Singer 1911 is $300K. A "Colt-pattern .45 pistol" on a probate inventory is $1,200. The difference walks out the door.

02

NFA paperwork stalls everything.

Without organized Form 1/4/5 status, tax-stamp numbers, and transferee tracking, an estate sits in probate for 12–24 months waiting on ATF.

03

The court inventory is unfit.

Florida Form P-3.0700 has no firearm-specific fields. Custom schedules end up handwritten, photocopied, and inconsistent across filings.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this legal advice?

No. EstateArms is software. It will not replace your probate attorney, your trust attorney, your CPA, or your FFL. It will give all of them better source data to work from.

Does this file Form 5 with ATF for me?

Not yet. EstateArms tracks every field a Form 5 requires — tax stamp number, transferor, transferee, dates — so the paperwork your NFA correspondent submits is correct on the first try. Direct e-filing is on the roadmap.

How is my estate data secured?

Each estate lives in an isolated SQLite database scoped to your account. Cloud-hosted tier uses encrypted-at-rest storage; self-hosted tier puts the database on your own server. We never share or sell inventory data. Period.

Can the AI identify firearms reliably?

For 95% of common items: yes, with rare-variant priority. For unique pieces, prototypes, or unmarked items, the AI provides candidate identifications and asks clarifying questions instead of guessing. Every identification can be overridden by a human appraiser.

What if I cancel mid-estate?

Your data is yours. Export the full inventory, the activity log, and every photo as a CSV/ZIP at any time on any tier (yes, including Free). No lock-in.

Can my probate attorney see the estate too?

On Professional and Firm tiers, yes — add their seat with view-only or edit access. On Executor tier, export the court-ready PDF and email it.

Why is the founder credible?
